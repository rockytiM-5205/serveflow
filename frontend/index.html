How it works

From order to serving, in three steps

ServeFlow keeps ordering and serving simple, so you can focus on what matters most: great food.

  1. 1. Customers order

    Customers open your link or scan your QR code, choose from your menu, pick dine-in or pickup and place the order from their phone. No app to install, and no account needed.

  2. 2. You confirm and cook

    Orders appear on your screen within seconds. Check your bank, confirm the payments (many at once when you are busy), and send the orders to the kitchen.

  3. 3. Customers are served

    Customers follow their order on their own phone. Pickup customers come at the time they chose, and dine-in guests are served at their table. You see who is waiting and who has paid.

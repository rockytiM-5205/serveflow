How it works

From order to serving, in a few steps

ServeFlow keeps ordering and serving simple, so you can focus on what matters most: great food.

  1. 1. Customers order

    Customers open your link or scan your QR code, choose from your menu and place an order from their phone. No app to install, no account to make.

  2. 2. You confirm and cook

    Orders appear on your screen within seconds. Check your bank, confirm the payments (many at once when you are busy), and send the orders to the kitchen.

  3. 3. Customers collect

    Customers follow their order on their own phone and come when it is ready. You see who is waiting and who has paid.
